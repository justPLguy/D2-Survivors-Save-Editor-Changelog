## What it is
Paste your save code, edit your heroes, gear, stash, gold and progress, then paste it back into the game. Nothing to install. It works on phone and desktop and comes in 6 languages: English, Deutsch, Français, 한국어, Polski and 中文.

The editor generates items **exactly the way the game does**, so what you make stays **legit**.

## Features
- **Hero:** level, stats, skills and passives, belt and potions, wings and Cain's quests, Golden Bow, progress from Normal up to Terror.
- **Items:** gear, inventory, charms, the stash (personal and 6 shared tabs), plus a **Create item** page. It makes magic, rare, unique and set items, all 94 runewords and jewels with stat ranges. A multi-threaded search finds the rolls you want.
- **Temper:** Charsi's forge with the current game rules, including the Hellforge.
- **Economy:** gold, Charsi's requirements and Charsi levels.
- **Tools:** Max your account in one click, a save check that marks every item legit or not legit, an encyclopedia, a save manager and Undo.

## Good to know
- **Edited saves never reach the ladder.** The game marks edited saves, and runs with them don't count towards leaderboards. The editor is made for offline fun and for testing builds. Keep your original code.
- Your save never leaves your browser unless you choose **Share** after an export. Sharing never sends your nickname.
